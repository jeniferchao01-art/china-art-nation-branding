CONTACT US

If you are an academic, a practitioner from the museum industry or a (nation) branding specialist, please join our network.

We organise interdisciplinary discussions and seminars, collaborate on research grants, and visit art exhibits in China and in the UK.

We also initiate PhD projects and conduct joint supervision. If you are a potential PhD candidate and would like to discuss your proposed project on Chinese contemporary art, popular culture and nation branding, please do not hesitate to email us.

You can use the contact form or reach us via WeChat. We will contact you only in connection with this network. We will not share your personal information.
