ORGANISERS:

Jenifer Chao

Specialising in global visual cultures, Jenifer Chao is Reader/Associate Professor at De Montfort University. She has published on art, popular culture and media in various academic journals and is the author of Cultural Resistance, 9/11, and the War on Terror: Sensible Interventions (Routledge, 2018).

Jiang Jiehong

Professor Jiang Jiehong is Director of the Centre for Chinese Visual Arts at Birmingham City University, and Principal Editor of the Journal of Contemporary Chinese Art (Intellect). Jiang has extensive research and curatorial experiences in contemporary art and visual culture.

Christopher Browning

Christopher Browning is Reader in Politics and International Studies at the University of Warwick. His research focuses on critical approaches to security and the politics of identity. He has written a number of research papers on nation branding and is currently writing a book on Nation Branding and International Politics.
